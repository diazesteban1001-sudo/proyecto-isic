## Citas literales que el proyecto usa

premios. `CLAUDE.md` y `PLAN.md` citan los dos nombres:

> Two Secondary Prizes: $7,500 each for "Top-15 Retrieval Sensitivity" and
> "Model Efficiency". Refer to Secondary Prize Metrics page for details.

"Sobre el problema"):

> Data other than the Competition Data ("External Data") may be used to

> available and equally accessible to use by all participants of the
> Competition for purposes of the competition at no cost to the other
> participants.
